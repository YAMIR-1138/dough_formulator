The Lab

Four experiments in what a formula could feel like. Same math, different instruments.

  • Ⅰ

    The Living Recipe

    Your formula written as prose you can touch. Tap any number in the sentence and the whole document - ingredients, schedule, everything - rewrites itself around you.
  • Ⅱ

    Dough Canvas

    The dough as a picture, not a spreadsheet. Ingredients stack in a glass jar; drag the layers to change the ratios, pull the surface to scale the batch.
  • Ⅲ

    Mission Control

    Built around when, not how much. Your bake is a ribbon of time - drag the bake to Sunday morning and the schedule reflows; warm the kitchen and watch bulk shrink.
  • Ⅳ

    Pocket Dial

    One decision at a time, one big satisfying dial per screen. Swipe from batch to hydration to schedule and end on a bake card that stays with you all day.
